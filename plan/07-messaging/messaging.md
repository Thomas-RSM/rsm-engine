# Messaging (v1)

Step 7 of the roadmap. 23 Nov 2026. Every page, post and profile speaks from this file.
Facts come from `02-facts/fact-register.md`.

## 1. Who RSM is, in one sentence

Relevant Search Media makes a business the answer its customers get, on Google and on every AI engine,
using the D'Souza Equation.

## 2. Who it is for

The owner of a US business who depends on being found: the plumber, the dentist, the law firm, the
roofer, the store. Not a marketing department. The owner is busy, has usually paid for search work
before, and was not shown what was done.

## 3. The core message

> Engines change. Your customer's question does not.
> We make you the most relevant answer to it, everywhere it is asked.

## 4. The equation, said the same way every time

> **The D'Souza Equation: Relevance = Read × Confirm × Choose.**
> Every engine names the business it can read plainly, confirm from other sources, and see real people
> choose. Miss one and the answer is zero.

## 5. The promise

Relevant search, relevant traffic, relevant leads.

## 6. Proof points, used in this order

1. **The method is public.** The D'Souza Standard: 413 checks, published in full.
2. **Two commitments in writing.** Every item ships and is logged, or that 15-day period is free. Half your mapped searches on page one of Google by the end of month 4, or we keep working at no charge.
3. **The record.** 900+ businesses served. About 400 projects, 90% successful. In digital marketing since 2010.
4. **The pace.** Live work by day 5. A report every 15 days. Work every day, each item logged with its link.
5. **The team.** 100+ specialists on US hours.
6. **The rule.** One business per trade per city.

## 7. Why book the call

The call is 20 to 30 minutes with Thomas. The owner leaves with three things about their own business:

1. **Who AI names** for their trade in their city today.
2. **Which customer searches they are missing**, across the whole map, not ten keywords.
3. **Whether their trade is still open** in their city.

It is a look at their position, not a sales pitch and not free work.

## 9. The hook for each kind of page

| Page type | The owner arrives thinking | The hook answers with |
|---|---|---|
| Home | "Who are these people?" | The core message, then the Ask field |
| Situation | "Something is wrong and I don't know what" | The answer to their question in the first two lines, then the three most likely causes |
| Equation | "How does this really work?" | The equation, then what it means for a business like theirs |
| Service | "I need SEO" or "I need a website" | What the service is, in plain words, and where it sits in the equation |
| Guide | "I want to understand before I spend" | The direct answer, then a verdict: do it yourself, hire anyone, or this is daily work |
| Mirror | "Show me my own business" | Their trade and city, straight away |
| Identity | "Can I trust you?" | Facts, the commitments, and the person behind them |

## 10. How every page handles terms

1. The title, heading and opening answer use the term the owner searched: SEO, AEO, GEO, local SEO, web design.
2. The answer is complete on its own.
3. Then the page shows where that term sits in the equation: which of Read, Confirm or Choose it serves.
4. The equation is named in full on first mention and links to its page.

## 11. Voice

- Plain words, the way an experienced owner talks to another owner.
- Short sentences. One idea each.
- "You" for the reader. "We" only for what RSM actually does.
- Say what a thing is before saying why it matters.
- A clear verdict where the reader needs one.
- No hype words, no filler, no fear. No "in today's digital world", "unlock", "elevate", "game-changer", "cutting-edge", "seamless".
- No client names. Results by trade and service only.
- Location stated once: New Delhi, India. Every page is written for the United States.
