# Keyword map v2

Step 5 of the roadmap. 23 Nov 2026. Built by `map_v2.py` and `woven.py`; raw Google answers are in `evidence/`.

## How each page is keyed

- **Locked, up to three per page.** One lead keyword that sets the address, title and heading, and up to two with the same intent. No other page may target them.
- **Woven, not locked.** Up to three section keywords and up to eight real questions, taken from Google's own suggestions. The writer uses the ones that fit; a question may be answered on only one page.

## Result

- 66 pages. 165 locked keywords. No keyword sits on two pages.
- Live evidence: 155 confirmed real searches, 7 weak signals, 0 without a signal, 3 RSM's own terms.
- 476 woven keywords: 139 section keywords and 337 questions.
- 9 pages have no search target by design: tools, trust and legal pages.

## Limits

- No search volumes. Autocomplete proves a search exists, not how often it is made. Volumes come from Search Console after launch.
- The checking machine is not always treated as a US user. Suggestions naming other countries are filtered out by script.
- The woven list is a pool. A few suggestions have the wrong intent and are dropped by the writer.
