# Page blueprints (v1)

Step 8 of the roadmap. 23 Nov 2026. One blueprint per page type: what goes on the page and in what
order. The hero for every page is in `heroes.md`. Messaging rules are in `07-messaging/messaging.md`.

## Parts every page has

| Part | Rule |
|---|---|
| Live strip | Standard version and date · Open Log day count · "Is your trade open in your city?" |
| Header | Five menus, the Ask field, the "Book a discovery call" button |
| Hero | H1, subline, primary button, lighter step, trust line |
| Trust line under the hero | 900+ businesses served · 100+ specialists on US hours · In digital marketing since 2010 |
| Breadcrumb | On every page below home |
| Author line | Thomas D'Souza, with published and last-reviewed dates |
| Closing block | The reason to book the call, the button, the lighter step |
| Footer | Plain list of every foundation page, contact, legal |

## The five blocks, on every answering page

1. **Answer.** The question as the heading and a direct answer in 40 to 60 words.
2. **Explain.** Why it is true, what changed, sources.
3. **Check yourself.** Three to five things the owner can check in ten minutes.
4. **How RSM solves it.** Where this sits in the D'Souza Equation, and what we do, in order, with timing.
5. **Next step.** Book a discovery call, and one lighter step.

## Blueprint: Home

1. Hero: the core message and the Ask field.
2. The two commitments, in two cards.
3. The D'Souza Equation: the formula, one line for each of Read, Confirm and Choose, link to the section.
4. "What's wrong?": the five situations as five doors.
5. Look yourself up: Index, Open Board, Searches, Your Plan.
6. The record: 900+ businesses, about 400 projects with 90% successful, 100+ specialists, since 2010.
7. How it starts: the five days, then the 15-day report.
8. Services, by their common names, in two groups: being found, and being built.
9. Results by trade, no names.
10. Thomas: photo, one paragraph, link.
11. Closing block.

## Blueprint: Situation page

1. Hero: the owner's question as the H1.
2. Answer block.
3. The likely causes, as a table: cause, how to tell, which part of the equation it is.
4. Check yourself.
5. Explain: how this happens, with one real pattern from RSM's work, by trade.
6. How RSM solves it: the order of fixes, with days.
7. Verdict: fix it yourself, hire anyone, or this is daily work.
8. Related problems and the matching service.
9. FAQ: five to eight real questions.
10. Closing block.

## Blueprint: Equation page

1. Hero: the term people search, tied to its part of the equation.
2. Answer block: what the term means in plain words.
3. The equation, with this page's part highlighted.
4. What every engine looks for here, as a table by engine group.
5. The checks: the groups of the D'Souza Standard that belong to this part, with counts and a link.
6. This page, scored against its own checks, with the date.
7. Check yourself.
8. How RSM does it.
9. FAQ.
10. Closing block. Lighter step: see all 413 checks.

## Blueprint: Service page

1. Hero: the service by its common name.
2. Answer block: what it is and who needs it.
3. Where it sits in the equation: Read, Confirm or Choose.
4. What is included, as a list that links deeper.
5. How it works: the first 15 days, item by item.
6. Proof: results by trade, the commitments.
7. How everyone should do it, and how we do it: a two-column table.
8. What it costs in the market and what a buyer should get. No RSM prices.
9. By trade and by city: links to the industry and city pages.
10. FAQ.
11. Closing block.

Build services (web design, web development, app development, custom CRM, design) use the same
blueprint, with "how it works" as the build stages and section 3 explaining how a site or app is built
so every engine can read it.

## Blueprint: Element page

1. Hero: the element by the name people search.

4. What good looks like, with a real example.
5. Check yours in ten minutes.
6. How everyone should do it, and how RSM does it.
7. The checks: the numbered checks of the D'Souza Standard that cover it.
8. Related elements, and the service that includes it.
9. FAQ.
10. Closing block. Lighter step: see all 413 checks.
