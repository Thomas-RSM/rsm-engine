# Stamping the theory: where it is published, and in what order

Step 1. Purpose: a public, dated, independent record that the theory is Thomas's, and a first wave of
places where people and AI engines will meet it. This adds to the 100-site catalogue in
`seo/off-site/` on the off-site branch; sites already in that catalogue are marked (C).

## Rules, no exceptions

1. **One canonical text.** One version-numbered paper on relevantsearchmedia.com. Everything else points to it.
2. **One definition sentence**, identical everywhere.
3. **One author name, one spelling**, everywhere.
4. **Every other piece is a native rewrite** for its platform, never a copy. Copies get accounts closed and add nothing.
5. **The stamp comes before the spread.** Nothing in wave 2 goes out until wave 1 is complete and logged.
6. **Every posting is logged**: place, URL, date, version, archive link.
7. **No client names, no unverified numbers, no invented quotes** in any piece.
