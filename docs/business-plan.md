# RSM Answer-to-Lead Business Plan

18 Nov 2026 · Relevant Search Media · source: https://claude.ai/artifact/WFSRUzFMtjimmhwnYreafn

## The idea

RSM stops being an agency site with a blog and becomes the place where a business owner's search-marketing question ends. Every page answers one question completely, shows how RSM would solve the problem behind it, and offers one next step.

The business is the gap between the answer and the doing. An owner who reads how to rank a plumbing business on ChatGPT in Miami learns two things: what has to be done, and that it is daily work they do not have time for. RSM gives away the first and sells the second.

Three things make this different from Search Engine Journal, Search Engine Land and Search Engine Roundtable. They write for marketers; RSM writes for the business owner, by trade and by city. They report news; RSM adds its own data, the AI Recommendation Index. They earn from advertising; RSM earns from the reader becoming a client.

## What makes the site unlike any other

Pages do not make a site stand out in 2026: anyone can generate ten thousand of them, and Google now penalises sites that do. What cannot be copied is anything that needs real work behind it every day. The site is built around four such things, and the library of pages sits behind them.

1. **The Ask box.** The home page opens with one field: "Ask anything about getting found online." The owner types a question and gets a short answer drawn from RSM's own pages, with the page for their trade and city and the next step. The site behaves like an answer engine, not a brochure.
2. **The AI Recommendation Index.** Every month RSM asks ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity who they recommend for each trade in each city, and publishes the results. No agency or publisher has this data. Owners come to find themselves in it; the press has a reason to cite it.
3. **The Open Log.** RSM runs this whole plan on its own site in public: what was published today, which searches it appears for, how often AI names it, how many calls were booked. Agencies show polished case studies; none shows its own numbers moving day by day. It is also the answer to the trust problem, because it cannot be faked for long.
4. **The Open Board.** A map of every trade in every launch city marked open or taken, from the one-business-per-trade-per-city rule. It makes the offer scarce and specific at a glance.

The Time Machine, the industry answers and the keyword chains are the library these four draw on. A visitor remembers the four; Google and the AI assistants read the library.

## The machine: five blocks on every page

Every page on the site, from a sales page to a news post, runs the same five blocks in the same order. This is what turns an answer into a lead.

1. **Answer.** The question in the heading and a direct answer in 40 to 60 words under it. This is the part Google and AI assistants lift.
2. **Explain.** Why the answer is true: how it works, what changed since the old answer, and the sources.
3. **Check yourself.** Three to five things the owner can look at in ten minutes to see whether they have this problem. Real, usable, do-it-yourself steps.
4. **How RSM solves it.** The exact steps RSM takes for this problem, in order, with timing: live work by day 5, report at day 15, the page-one commitment at month 4.
5. **Next step.** One action only: book 20 minutes with Thomas. Beside it, a second and lighter step for people not ready to talk (see the lead section below).

Blocks 1 to 3 make RSM the answer. Blocks 4 and 5 make it a business. A page missing block 3 reads like an advert; a page missing block 4 gives everything away and asks for nothing.

## How the lead is caught

One main action and four devices, all built from things RSM already has. None of them is free work, which keeps to the existing rule of no free audit and no free trial.

- **Book 20 minutes with Thomas.** The single main action on every page, through the existing Calendly link.
- **The open-or-taken check.** RSM takes one business per trade per city. Every city and industry page shows whether that place is open, for example "Plumbers in Miami: open". It is true, it is specific to the reader, and it gives a reason to act now.
- **The AI Recommendation Index.** A public page per trade per city showing which businesses ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity name. The owner looks up their own trade, sees whether they are named, and can leave an email to be told when the list changes.
- **The daily brief.** One email a day with that day's changes in Google and AI search. It keeps owners who are not ready to buy within reach.
- **The chat widget** already on the site, for questions that come up while reading.

Every lead records the page it came from, so each keyword can be judged by calls booked and not by visits.

## How RSM explains its solution

Block 4 on every page uses the same six facts, applied to that page's problem. They are what RSM does that the reader cannot easily get elsewhere.

1. Live work by day 5.
2. A 313-point audit at the start.
3. Work every day, recorded in a log the client can read.
4. A report every 15 days.
5. An AI Score measured across six AI engines.
6. Two written commitments: every item ships and is logged or that 15-day period is free; half the mapped searches reach Google page one by month 4 or the work continues free until they do.

Prices are not shown. Cost pages explain what the market charges and what a buyer should get for it, then show these six facts as the standard to hold any agency to.

The explanation is specific to the page. On a fix-it page about a suspended Google listing, block 4 lists the reinstatement steps RSM takes. On a plumber page in Miami, it lists what gets done for a plumber in the first 15 days.

## The numbers

No baseline exists yet: current traffic, lead sources and close rate are not known, so this plan sets the formula and the first measurements, not a forecast.

```latex
\text{new clients per month} = \text{visits} \times \frac{\text{booked calls}}{\text{visits}} \times \frac{\text{clients}}{\text{booked calls}}
```

Monthly income from a client is their plan fee (Essential $399, Accelerate $599, Partnership $999), for as many months as they stay. Four numbers therefore run the business, and month one exists to measure them:

| Number | Where it comes from | Known today |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visits to pages with a booking button | Google Search Console and Analytics | No |
| Booked calls per 100 visits | Calendly, by source page | No |
| Clients per 10 booked calls | Thomas's call log | No |
| Months an average client stays | Billing records for the 900 past clients | No |

Example only, to show how the formula reads: 5,000 visits at 1 call per 100 visits is 50 calls; at 2 clients per 10 calls that is 10 new clients a month. The real rates replace these once month one is measured.

The research adds one caution. AI answers now cut clicks to websites by roughly 38% to 58% on searches where they appear, so visits will understate reach. Being named in the AI answer is counted separately, as how often RSM appears over repeated runs.

## The daily operation

Each day covers one trade in one city: 8 pages on the site and 12 pieces off it, as set out in the 60-day calendar. Every page carries one main keyword and at most two supporting ones.

| Step | What happens | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research | Run the AI prompts for that trade and city; gather three local facts with sources | Researcher |
| Draft | Write the five blocks for each of the 8 pages | Writer, with AI drafting |
| Check | Facts, sources, local detail, that block 4 matches what RSM really does | Editor; nothing publishes unchecked |
| Publish | Pages go live with their links and schema | Web team |
| Distribute | The 12 off-site pieces, each made from one of the 8 pages | Social team |
| Record | Thomas records one session a week for video and quotes | Thomas |

Who fills each role, and how many pages a day one editor can check properly, is not yet known. That number, not the calendar, sets the real pace.

## Risks and the rule for each

| Risk | Why it is real | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google treats the pages as mass-produced | 480 pages in 60 days on a new site; Google's 2024 spam policies name scaled content and city doorway pages | Every city page carries three facts true only for that city and trade. A page that reads the same with the city swapped is not published. |
| Volume outruns checking | AI drafts faster than a person can verify | Nothing publishes unchecked. If checking falls behind, the daily count drops, not the standard. |
| Wrong facts in Time Machine pages | Dates and figures were gathered from search summaries, not primary pages | Each fact is confirmed at its source before its page goes live. |
| No US office | Google map listings in US cities are closed to RSM | Target normal results and AI answers; never use a virtual address. |
| Trust | No visible reviews, mismatched company facts, one prospect already lost on trust | The trust list above is finished before day 1. |
| Off-site posting read as spam | Copies of one article on many sites, or automated links, get accounts banned | Off-site pieces are native rewrites on RSM's own profiles; Reddit and Quora get full answers, links only where allowed. |
| AI answers take the click | Fewer visits even when RSM is the source | Count AI mentions as a result; put the answer and the next step at the top of every page. |
| One public face | Everything is in Thomas's name | Add named authors from the team as the content grows. |

## The first 90 days, in order

1. **Before day 1: foundation.** Finish the trust list. Launch the new site with the 461 skeleton pages: home, national service and industry pages, and each launch city's main pages. Connect Search Console, Analytics and booking-source tracking.
2. **Days 1 to 60: the posting run.** One trade and one city a day, 8 pages on the site and 12 pieces off it: six trades across ten cities, 60 industry answers, 60 Time Machine pages.
3. **Day 30: first reading.** Measure the four numbers. Check which pages are indexed and which bring booked calls. Fix the page template before writing 240 more pages on it.
4. **Day 60: decide the next phase.** Open the next nine trades only where the first pages are indexed and earning impressions. Where they are not, improve those pages first.
5. **Days 61 to 90: proof and reach.** Publish the first case studies and the first monthly AI Recommendation Index report, and pitch that report to trade and local press.
