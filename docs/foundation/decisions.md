## The method

- **Fixed.** RSM presents itself as the originator of its own method. Content shows this through a dated standard, an origin page and public records. It is shown, not boasted.
- **Fixed.** The meaning of the company name is the outcome of the method: **relevant searches, relevant traffic, relevant leads**.
- **Proposed.** The measured proof is the **Relevance Rate**: the share of a client's leads the client confirms were relevant, reported every 15 days.
- **Proposed.** Name of the method: **Universal Engine Optimization (UEO)**. Checked 22 Nov 2026: no existing use found in search marketing. Not a trademark clearance.
- **Fixed.** Differentiator to state everywhere: RSM maps and works every kind of search a business should appear for, across Google, AI answers, AI recommendations and social networks. It does not target 10 or 15 keywords.
- Background for writers: the engine idea draws on the Sri Yantra. One centre (the client's business), interlocking parts around it, power at the intersections. This is for understanding, not for the name or the graphics.

Names tested and ruled out because others use them: Relevance Engineering (iPullRank), Everysearch (Found), Search Everywhere Optimization (Siu and Patel), XEO, DEO, RSO (RSO Consulting), Omnirank, names built on "map" (On The Map Marketing, Miami).

## Term policy: existing terms and the new term

The existing terms bring the visitor in. The new term is what they leave with.

1. **Every page's main keyword is an existing term with demand**, taken from the keyword universe. The new term is the main keyword only on its own pages: what it is, how it compares, the Standard and the origin page.
2. **Title, URL, H1 and the 40 to 60 word answer use the existing term.** The answer must be complete without mentioning the method.
3. **The new term appears in the "How RSM solves it" block**, written in full on first mention with the short form after it, with one link to its definition page.
4. **One definition sentence, identical everywhere**: on the site, in schema, in Thomas's profiles and in every off-site piece.
5. **Menu and internal link text stay as the existing keyword.** The new term never replaces a term people search.
6. **No existing term with demand is left without a home.** The coverage table below is checked whenever pages are added.
7. **Spreading the term.** Every blog, article, video and social post uses the same pattern: answer the common question under its common name, then name the method. Thomas is credited as its author each time.
8. **Measuring the spread.** Monthly: searches for the term in Search Console, and what ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity answer when asked what it is. Both go in the Open Log.
