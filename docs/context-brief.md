## The business idea
RSM becomes the place where a business owner's search-marketing question ends. Every page answers one
question completely, shows how RSM would solve the problem behind it, and offers one next step.
It gives away the answer and sells the daily doing.

## Four living things the site is built around (only RSM can have them)
1. **Ask box** — home page opens with one field: "Ask anything about getting found online."
2. **AI Recommendation Index** — monthly: who ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity recommend per trade per city.
3. **Open Log** — RSM runs the plan on its own site in public (published today, searches, AI mentions, calls).
4. **Open Board** — every trade in every launch city marked open or taken (one business per trade per city).
Behind them: the library (encyclopedia, Time Machine, industry answers, keyword chains).

## Five blocks on every page, in order
Answer (40–60 words) → Explain → Check yourself (3–5 things in ten minutes) → How RSM solves it → Next step
(book 20 minutes with Thomas, plus one lighter step).

## Four kinds of page
How does this work? (guide / answer / Time Machine) · Why is this broken? (fix-it) ·
Who should I pick? (comparison / proof) · I want to hire (sales page).
