01 · The D'Souza Equation

How does a search engine optimization company in the USA make you the answer?

With one equation that every engine obeys. Google, Maps, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity look different, but each does one job: take a person's question and name the most relevant business it can trust.

They differ in how they read. They agree on what they are looking for.

Relevance = Read × Confirm × Choose

42=80×70×75

The three terms multiply. If any one is zero, the result is zero.

    10 · Questions owners ask

    Questions owners ask before they call

    Asked on discovery calls, answered here the same way.

    For anything else, the Ask page finds the page that answers it.

    How many SEO agencies are there in the USA?

    Tens of thousands. Most keep their method private and show you a deck.

    Relevant Search Media publishes its method as the D'Souza Standard, 413 numbered checks, so you can hold any agency, including us, to the same list. That is the difference to look for, not the count.

    What is a search engine optimization company in the USA supposed to do?

    Make your business the answer to the searches your customers make. In 2026 that means Google, Google Maps and the AI engines together. We map every search for your trade and city, run 413 checks, fix the biggest things first, and log each item with its link, so you can see the work every day.

    Is there an affordable SEO company in the USA that still does real work?

    Affordable means the work is worth what it costs, and you can see it. Every plan is month to month, with no setup fee and nothing sold extra.

    Live work goes up by day 5, each item is logged with its link, and a report arrives every 15 days. Prices are discussed on the discovery call.

    How soon will I see results?

    Live work goes up on day 5 and your first report arrives on day 15. Rankings usually start moving between day 30 and day 60. By the end of month 4, at least half the customer searches in your map reach page one of Google, or we keep working at no charge until they do.

    Can you get my business recommended by ChatGPT?

    We do the work the AI engines use to choose a business: facts they can read, independent sources that confirm them, and signs that real people choose you. No one can honestly promise a placement in an AI answer, so we track where you are named, re-run the AI Recommendation Index every month, and report it.

    Will you also work with my competitors?

    No. We take one business per trade per city.

    Two roofers in the same city cannot both be the business an engine names, so your slot is yours for as long as you stay. The Open Board shows which trades and cities are taken.

    Can I cancel, and what do I keep?

    Every plan is month to month. You cancel by email.

    You keep everything: the website, the content, the listings and every account, from day one. Nothing is held back and nothing is charged to leave.

    Is Relevant Search Media an SEO agency in the USA or in India?

    Both, honestly. The company is registered in New Delhi, India, and the whole team works US hours.

    Every client is a US business, and every search we map is a US search. You get an SEO agency in the USA by market and by working day, at a cost structure that lets us keep every plan month to month.
