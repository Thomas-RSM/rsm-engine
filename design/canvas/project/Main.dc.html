02The D'Souza Equation

Engines change. Your customer's question does not.

Google, Maps, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity look different, but each does one job: take a person's question and name the most relevant business it can trust. They differ in how they read. They agree on what they are looking for.

Relevance = Read × Confirm × Choose The three terms multiply. If any one is zero, the result is zero.
